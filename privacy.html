Personal Google Drive Integration

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Overview

This policy describes how the owner uses a personal Google OAuth application with rclone to access the owner's Google Drive. The integration is for personal use and is not a public service.

Information accessed

Depending on the permissions granted during Google authorization, rclone may access Google Drive files and metadata needed to list, read, upload, modify, or manage files. The owner configures rclone and determines how the integration is used.

How information is used

Information accessed through Google Drive is used for the owner's personal file-management and media-library workflows on the owner's server. This website itself does not request Google sign-in and does not collect Google Drive files or OAuth credentials.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials and any locally cached file data may be stored on the owner's server according to the rclone configuration. The owner does not intentionally sell personal information or share data accessed through this integration with third parties, except where needed to operate the services the owner has configured or where required by law.

Third-party services

Google Drive is provided by Google and is subject to Google's terms and privacy policy. rclone is third-party open-source software. Use of those services and software is also subject to their applicable terms and policies.

Security and retention

The owner is responsible for protecting the server, configuration files, OAuth credentials, and any local cache. Data remains in Google Drive unless the owner or configured software changes or deletes it. Local cache data may be retained or removed according to the server configuration.

Your choices and contact

This is a personal integration, not a service for public users. For questions or requests concerning this application, contact the owner at: yogi.317.ys@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the integration's purpose or data handling changes. The date above will be updated when material changes are made.
